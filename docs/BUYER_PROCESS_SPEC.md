# Buyer transaction process: client-approved specification

Source: client's own marked-up diagram (received 2026-11-13). This is the **right process** according to the client.
It applies to the **Buyer, Broker and Agent portals**. The **Supplier portal is different** and will be sent separately.

Every step is a back-and-forth between the Buyer portal and Admin (Finekarts).
Every step on both sides must allow upload, download and signing of documents.
On the Admin side, staff must be able to update and correct any document Finekarts generated, and must have access to the fully formatted version of every generated document.

## Starting point (confirmed by the client on 2026-11-13)

The process starts when Finekarts sends the buyer an **SCO or FCO**: the product offer with price per MT and Incoterms.
This is Finekarts' offer to the buyer. The buyer **accepts it by submitting the ICPO**. That is why the ICPO is irrevocable.
From there the parties proceed to the contract (the PSA).
So the ICPO should be based on the SCO/FCO the buyer received (pre-filled where possible), not a blank form.

## The 7 steps

| # | Admin (Finekarts) | Buyer portal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generate a pre-loaded SCO or FCO with the product name and price (FOB and CIF). Send it to the buyer. | Buyer receives and reviews the SCO/FCO. Buyer submits a generated or pre-loaded ICPO or LOI, or uploads their own. |
| 2 | Receive and review the ICPO or LOI. Request CIS and RWA. Staff may make an exception for the FIS (see rules). The buyer may also upload ICPO/LOI, CIS, RWA and FIS all at once. | If the buyer did not upload RWA, CIS and FIS in step 1, the buyer sends them in this step. The RWA is used only for LC transactions. |
| 3 | Receive and review CIS, RWA, FIS. Generate the PSA automatically from the ICPO or LOI (must meet bank regulation). Send the PSA with the LC wording to the buyer to sign. | Buyer receives, reviews, signs and stamps the PSA. Attach a copy of the passport of the person in charge (not employees). Send to Finekarts. |
| 4 | Receive the signed and stamped PSA with the buyer's passport. Finekarts signs and stamps the PSA and attaches a copy of the passport of the person in charge (manager, president or owner, not employees). | Buyer receives a copy of the fully signed PSA. After the contract is signed, Finekarts also sends the Commercial Invoice (CI). |
| 5 | Generate a banking-regulated Commercial Invoice. Request the Letter of Credit type and exact amount agreed on the PSA. Everything must match. | Buyer receives the CI and creates the exact LC type agreed on the PSA. **The buyer must submit the LC from the issuing bank within 24 hours.** |
| 6 | Receive the LC. Send all contract paperwork to the banking advisor for review and approval. The bank opens an LC account to buy from the supplier. | If the buyer issued a faulty LC or one that does not match the PSA, the buyer pays the amendment on both banks. If the LC is good, there is no amendment to pay. |
| 7 | After collecting all LC documents and certificates agreed on the PSA, the advisor reviews all documents and sends them to the buyer's bank for payment. | The seller and bank submit all documents to the buyer's bank. The buyer's bank receives all shipping documents and sends the final payment via SWIFT MT103. |

## Rules

- Every month the issuer's bank must [handle/renew] the LC at the exact dates, same amount, same LC types. (Wording unclear in the diagram; confirm with the client.)
- The RWA is required on every LC transaction, regardless of small or large.
- A returning buyer's CIS and FIS are already on record. A new buyer uploads the RWA and the CIS.
- The FIS: Finekarts can make an exception depending on the situation. In large LC transactions the FIS is definitely required.

## Differences from what was built before this spec (to fix)

1. Step 1 starts with Finekarts sending an SCO/FCO to the buyer, then the buyer submits ICPO/LOI. The buyer-only tabs started at the ICPO.
2. The deadline to submit the LC is **24 hours**, not 72 hours.
3. The FIS is required by default. Staff can make an exception. It is always required for large LC transactions. (An optional-FIS rule was built earlier; that was based on an earlier message and is superseded.)
4. The PSA is generated by Finekarts from the ICPO/LOI and sent with the LC wording. Finekarts also attaches the passport of the person in charge when it counter-signs.
5. Step 6 covers faulty LC and amendment cost; step 7 covers the shipping documents and MT103 payment. Neither exists in the portal today.
6. Admin needs one place per transaction that shows these steps with download, upload, sign and edit-generated-document at each step.
