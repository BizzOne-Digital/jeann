Finekarts — commercial document checklist (educational)
========================================================

Use this as a starting point for buyer–seller discussions. Actual document sets vary by product, corridor, bank, and contract.

Commercial
- Commercial invoice (aligned with contract and LC)
- Packing list (weights, marks, packages)
- Proforma invoice (if used pre-shipment)

Transport
- Bill of lading or sea waybill
- Charter party references (bulk programmes)

Origin & compliance
- Certificate of origin
- Phytosanitary / health certificates (as required)

Quality & quantity
- Certificate of analysis
- Certificate of weight / quantity
- Independent inspection certificate (mutually agreed inspector)

Not legal advice. Confirm requirements with your bank and advisers.
